Biography

I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Statistics at the University of Michigan, where I am very fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Ji Zhu and Prof. Gongjun Xu.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Beijing Normal University in 2025, where I was very fortunate to be advised by Prof. Lixing Zhu. Before my doctoral studies, I completed my undergraduate education in Statistics at Beijing Normal University, graduating in 2020.

Research Interests

Complex Data Structures

Statistical inference for high-dimensional, tensor, functional, and other structured data.

Model Checking and Testing

Specification testing, goodness-of-fit testing, and model-structure detection for complex models.

Change Point Detection

Dimension-reduction and low-rank methods for detecting structural changes in high-dimensional and tensor data.

Statistical Machine Learning

Statistical machine learning and generative modeling methods with theoretical guarantees.
