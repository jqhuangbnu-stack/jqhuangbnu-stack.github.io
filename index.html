Biography

Statistical inference, model-structure detection, high-dimensional data, and machine learning with statistical guarantees.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Statistics at the University of Michigan, working with Prof. Ji Zhu and Prof. Gongjun Xu. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Beijing Normal University, where I was advised by Prof. Lixing Zhu. Before my doctoral studies, I completed my undergraduate education in Statistics at Beijing Normal University.

Statistical Inference Specification Testing Change Point Detection High-Dimensional Data Generative Modeling

Research Interests

Complex Data Structures

Statistical methods for high-dimensional, tensor, functional, and structured data.

Model Checking and Testing

Specification testing, goodness-of-fit testing, and model-structure detection.

Change Point Detection

Dimension-reduction and low-rank methods for detecting structural changes.

Statistical Machine Learning

Machine learning and generative modeling methods with statistical guarantees.
